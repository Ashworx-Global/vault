# Encountering New Religious Movements — Hexham, Rost & Morehead (eds.)

**Editors:** Irving Hexham, Stephen Rost & John W. Morehead II
(Calibre lists Hexham as author; it is an edited volume with contributors
from the US, Canada and Australia)
**Publisher:** Kregel Academic, 2004
**Text:** Scripture quotes default NIV (some KJV/NASB/NKJV) — wording
differs from ESV
**Coverage warning:** this EPUB only contains the Foreword, Introduction,
Chapters 1–2 and part of Chapter 3 (of 13 chapters + Conclusion).
Chapters 4–13 are listed below from the contents but NOT summarized —
read them in full.

## Thesis

Shift from traditional counter-cult apologetics (rational, confrontational
refutation of error) to holistic, incarnational, contextual missiology:
enter new religionists' worldviews with empathy and long-term relationship,
do cultural exegesis alongside Scriptural exegesis, and treat NRMs as
unreached people-groups needing cross-cultural mission — "share the gospel
*with* people, not preach *at* them." Apologetics stays, but as necessary,
not sufficient. Deliberately says "new religious movements," not "cults"
(pejorative, a stumbling block).

## What's inside (full contents)

- Foreword — Gailyn Van Rheenen (incarnation over ridicule; contact AND
  contrast, per Hesselgrave)

  North America

## Double-check while reading

- **"Cult" vs NRM definition:** editors admit competing evangelical
  definitions; their Melton-plus-creeds formula is a proposal — test it.
- **Numbers (700–1,000) and growth causes** (modernization, secularization,
  globalization) — debated; check Melton, Jenkins, Stark/Finke.
- **"Apologetic refutation doesn't work":** based on pioneers' field
  experience; Part 3 claims the only tested models, and editors admit gaps
  (no qualified authors for some major groups).
- **Common ground limits:** Hesselgrave (Foreword) warns similarities can
  mislead — dissimilarities matter more long-term; incorporating e.g. oils
  doesn't Christianize aromatherapy.
- **Celtic revival:** critics (Bradley, Robinson, Bowman, Meek) call much of
  it romanticized recent construction — verify Hunter/Finney claims.
- **Zwemer/Lull/Francis narratives:** hagiography risk; check Dries,
  Mallouhi, Vander Werff, Parshall as cited.
- **Ch 3's Lonergan solution:** extract ends before it lands — read the
  print/complete ebook for the payoff on method wars.
- **Getting the missing two-thirds:** this file stops mid–Ch 3; everything
  after (global cultures, Areopagus paradigm, all seven field models,
  conclusion) still needs reading.
