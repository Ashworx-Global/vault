❓ COMMON QUESTIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  Q: Do I need to export from Photoshop?
  A: No! Put PSDs in source/, they auto-convert to PNG (300 DPI).

  Q: What if I manually export PNG?
  A: Put in exports/, watermark still applied automatically.

  Q: Can I delete the sample project?
  A: Yes! It's just an example. Delete Art/sample-study-01/ when ready.

  Q: How do I change the watermark?
  A: Edit scripts/art_processor.py line ~110.

  Q: Can users bypass right-click protection?
  A: Yes, tech-savvy users can, but it discourages casual copying + watermark is always there.

  Q: Site not updating?
  A: Hard-refresh: Cmd+Shift+R (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows/Linux)
     GitHub build takes 2-3 minutes.
