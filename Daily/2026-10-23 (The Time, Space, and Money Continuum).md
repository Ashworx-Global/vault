*Thus says the Lord God: Enough, O princes of Israel! Put away violence and oppression, and execute justice and righteousness. Cease your evictions of my people, declares the Lord God. (Eze 45:9)*

God asks for a section to be set aside for Him, and then he declares to the leaders to act in a way the is honorable to the Lord, it is wise to heed His call here. Fathers heed the call to be like this to your families, managers heed the call to be like this to your staff.

*It shall be the prince’s duty to furnish the burnt offerings, grain offerings, and drink offerings, at the feasts (Eze 45:17)*

How vastly different is this instruction to what we see today, the taking from our leaders from the people, this appears to be the total opposite.

The prince shall not take any of the inheritance of the people, thrusting them out of their property. He shall give his sons their inheritance out of his own property, so that none of my people shall be scattered from his property.” (Eze 46:18)

Leaders may not take what is not theirs to take. If I think of what is happening, leaders are wanting to take land from its citizens in the disguise of taking back, but really its steal from teh people what Ezekiel seems to be pointing out. 

The operations and places for the temple, each have their own space and time, giving to God is far greater than just money, its giving in our actions, time and space. To God be the glory in all that we do.
