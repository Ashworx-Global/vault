  [[Mark]] [[Mark 6]]

	Immediately he made his disciples get into the boat and go before him (Mk 6:45)

This was pointed out to me by a prayer the other day, Jesus made them go, Jesus knew what was coming, He put them in the storm. There are moments when we are in the storm on purpose, it is our responsibility to keep on the Lord.

	He meant to pass by them, (Mk 6:48)

Now this is interesting, I have not seen this before, this is almost like when you have a son or daughter and when you can see that they are trying hard at something and inside you say “you got this” and you continue on, as you know that they can make it without you, it will be hard going for your child but you know it will be good for them to struggle a little. I get the picture in this one line.

	And he got into the boat with them, and the wind ceased. (Mk 6:51)

This is like when your child is struggling ,and then you quickly help and the struggle is gone, there is a calm.

Jesus, says it so plainly,
	And he said to them, “You have a fine way of rejecting the commandment of God in order to establish your tradition! ( Mk 7:9)
Often what we think is christian living can be religion and religion by its self is empty, reading Gods word and walking through what it says and going what it says is right.
