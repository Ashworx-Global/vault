We see the fall of a father, Solomon had deception in his heart because he walked away from God, it is unclear why ,but it does seem he surrounded himself with the wrong people and being swayed by them, he got prideful and followed other gods. His fall reached into his family line and his son fell deeper down that path making calves of gold as idols and making people worship it. We see Judah spared this, and through Judah we get Jesus. Keep God-fearing company and you will be strengthened.
