In this scene, the father of the afflicted boy says to Jesus, “If you can do anything, have compassion on us and help us,” and Jesus responds with “‘If you can’!” ([Mark 9:14–29](https://ref.ly/logosref/bible$2Besv.62.9.14-62.9.29)) This is Jesus echoing and challenging the father’s own words.

The Greek word behind “can” is **δύναμαι** (_dynamai_), meaning “to be able” or “to have power.” Jesus repeats the doubt expressed by the father to challenge it.[1](logos4-command:TextRangePreview%7CRanges%3D1089826%3A174%3AeyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJyZXNvdXJjZUlkIjoiTExTOlRITVJLU1RTVE1OVFNSUyIsInJlc291cmNlVmVyc2lvbiI6IjIwMjEtMDMtMzBUMTU6Mjg6MDdaIiwiaW5kZXhlZE9mZnNldCI6IjEwODk4MjYiLCJpbmRleGVkTGVuZ3RoIjoiMTc0IiwibmJmIjoxNzk0ODgzMzE4LCJleHAiOjE3OTQ4ODY5MTgsImlhdCI6MTc5NDg4MzMxOCwiaXNzIjoiVGV4dFJhbmdlQWNjZXNzVG9rZW4iLCJhdWQiOiIxMzkxNjE3MiJ9.Iy3h8EOnopwCSplQ2Kx9nXd2sMrDOJw1ssLYfEYQmdc%7CResourceId%3DLLS%3ATHMRKSTSTMNTSRS%7CResourceVersion%3D2021-03-30T15%3A28%3A07Z) The father’s phrasing places the burden on Jesus’s ability—essentially expressing doubt about whether Jesus has sufficient power. The father believes that Jesus would like to do something if he could, but he does not firmly believe that he can do anything in such a grim case. The father considers Jesus’ lack of power to be the obstacle to healing.[1](logos4-command:TextRangePreview%7CRanges%3D1089443%3A139%3AeyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJyZXNvdXJjZUlkIjoiTExTOlRITVJLU1RTVE1OVFNSUyIsInJlc291cmNlVmVyc2lvbiI6IjIwMjEtMDMtMzBUMTU6Mjg6MDdaIiwiaW5kZXhlZE9mZnNldCI6IjEwODk0NDMiLCJpbmRleGVkTGVuZ3RoIjoiMTM5IiwibmJmIjoxNzk0ODgzMzE5LCJleHAiOjE3OTQ4ODY5MTksImlhdCI6MTc5NDg4MzMxOSwiaXNzIjoiVGV4dFJhbmdlQWNjZXNzVG9rZW4iLCJhdWQiOiIxMzkxNjE3MiJ9.mk4uPQ5pJOKKztGCkDhuGoB2guQifiCo6s0th3cd51o%7CResourceId%3DLLS%3ATHMRKSTSTMNTSRS%7CResourceVersion%3D2021-03-30T15%3A28%3A07Z)

Jesus’s response redirects the emphasis entirely. The rhetorical move is sharp: Jesus isn’t denying his power but rather pivots to expose the father’s wavering faith. Unlike the disciples, Jesus possesses unlimited power as God’s Son.
