#### FROM LOGOS

“ And I will grant authority to my two witnesses, and they will prophesy for 1,260 days, clothed in sackcloth.” - Rev 11:3

I asked: “ Why 1260 days, is there something important for that number?”

The 1,260 days carries significance because it represents a specific unit of time that appears repeatedly across apocalyptic literature with consistent theological meaning. The 1,260 days corresponds to forty-two months (or thirty days each month)[1](logos4-command:TextRangePreview%7CRanges%3D1011850%3A118%3AeyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJyZXNvdXJjZUlkIjoiTExTOjk3ODE3ODE5MTE5NTIiLCJyZXNvdXJjZVZlcnNpb24iOiIyMDE5LTAzLTI4VDIyOjM3OjE0WiIsImluZGV4ZWRPZmZzZXQiOiIxMDExODUwIiwiaW5kZXhlZExlbmd0aCI6IjExOCIsIm5iZiI6MTc5MTk1MDc3NiwiZXhwIjoxNzkxOTU0Mzc2LCJpYXQiOjE3OTE5NTA3NzYsImlzcyI6IlRleHRSYW5nZUFjY2Vzc1Rva2VuIiwiYXVkIjoiMTM5MTYxNzIifQ.n-o6Im7xAlLK37RTbIMqW3uzTIhWftBFA-3vkqeqR0A%7CResourceId%3DLLS%3A9781781911952%7CResourceVersion%3D2019-03-28T22%3A37%3A14Z), and this calculation yields 3½ (1 + 2 + ½)[1](logos4-command:TextRangePreview%7CRanges%3D1011536%3A105%3AeyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJyZXNvdXJjZUlkIjoiTExTOjk3ODE3ODE5MTE5NTIiLCJyZXNvdXJjZVZlcnNpb24iOiIyMDE5LTAzLTI4VDIyOjM3OjE0WiIsImluZGV4ZWRPZmZzZXQiOiIxMDExNTM2IiwiaW5kZXhlZExlbmd0aCI6IjEwNSIsIm5iZiI6MTc5MTk1MDc3NywiZXhwIjoxNzkxOTU0Mzc3LCJpYXQiOjE3OTE5NTA3NzcsImlzcyI6IlRleHRSYW5nZUFjY2Vzc1Rva2VuIiwiYXVkIjoiMTM5MTYxNzIifQ.gufyo3ECWqPt-RbJsl6SPXw8Qw4WjkVlbsHSebfO7mo%7CResourceId%3DLLS%3A9781781911952%7CResourceVersion%3D2019-03-28T22%3A37%3A14Z)—a figure rooted in Daniel’s prophecies describing “a time, times, and half a time” ([Dan 7:25](https://ref.ly/logosref/bible$2Besv.27.7.25); [12:7](https://ref.ly/logosref/bible$2Besv.27.12.7)).

The repetition matters. This same period appears in Revelation as forty-two months (the nations trampling the holy city), 1,260 days (the two witnesses prophesying and the woman nourished in the wilderness), and “a time, times, and half a time” (the woman’s protection)[1](logos4-command:TextRangePreview%7CRanges%3D1011755%3A90%3AeyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJyZXNvdXJjZUlkIjoiTExTOjk3ODE3ODE5MTE5NTIiLCJyZXNvdXJjZVZlcnNpb24iOiIyMDE5LTAzLTI4VDIyOjM3OjE0WiIsImluZGV4ZWRPZmZzZXQiOiIxMDExNzU1IiwiaW5kZXhlZExlbmd0aCI6IjkwIiwibmJmIjoxNzkxOTUwNzc4LCJleHAiOjE3OTE5NTQzNzgsImlhdCI6MTc5MTk1MDc3OCwiaXNzIjoiVGV4dFJhbmdlQWNjZXNzVG9rZW4iLCJhdWQiOiIxMzkxNjE3MiJ9.kveM9BRZsEGQtweyqlubukW2UWXAXEhagA92yzxO0u4%7CResourceId%3DLLS%3A9781781911952%7CResourceVersion%3D2019-03-28T22%3A37%3A14Z). John’s vision in Revelation alludes to [Daniel 12](https://ref.ly/logosref/bible$2Besv.27.12), with the scroll John receives containing fuller revelation about the final period Daniel predicted—concerning how God’s kingdom transfers to “the one like a son of man” through the church’s faithful witness accompanied by suffering[2](logos4-command:TextRangePreview%7CRanges%3D240681%3A61%3AeyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJyZXNvdXJjZUlkIjoiTExTOlFTVE5TTkRUTVMiLCJyZXNvdXJjZVZlcnNpb24iOiIyMDE1LTAxLTA3VDIyOjE2OjAxWiIsImluZGV4ZWRPZmZzZXQiOiIyNDA2ODEiLCJpbmRleGVkTGVuZ3RoIjoiNjEiLCJuYmYiOjE3OTE5NTA3NzksImV4cCI6MTc5MTk1NDM3OSwiaWF0IjoxNzkxOTUwNzc5LCJpc3MiOiJUZXh0UmFuZ2VBY2Nlc3NUb2tlbiIsImF1ZCI6IjEzOTE2MTcyIn0.etLSLy_Vig2gUYCVcE4cpzQ_-_whn0m29oG9szze3gU%7CResourceId%3DLLS%3AQSTNSNDTMS%7CResourceVersion%3D2015-01-07T22%3A16%3A01Z).

Interpreters divide on whether this number is literal or symbolic. Some argue these designations are theological rather than chronological—indicating the quality or kind of time rather than its precise duration, emphasizing what kind of events occur rather than how long they last[1](logos4-command:TextRangePreview%7CRanges%3D1011015%3A356%3AeyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJyZXNvdXJjZUlkIjoiTExTOjk3ODE3ODE5MTE5NTIiLCJyZXNvdXJjZVZlcnNpb24iOiIyMDE5LTAzLTI4VDIyOjM3OjE0WiIsImluZGV4ZWRPZmZzZXQiOiIxMDExMDE1IiwiaW5kZXhlZExlbmd0aCI6IjM1NiIsIm5iZiI6MTc5MTk1MDc4MCwiZXhwIjoxNzkxOTU0MzgwLCJpYXQiOjE3OTE5NTA3ODAsImlzcyI6IlRleHRSYW5nZUFjY2Vzc1Rva2VuIiwiYXVkIjoiMTM5MTYxNzIifQ.UJOBYEF5i02WIEvATfkBs8KlFglXiJ2g1YUsLdGgrE8%7CResourceId%3DLLS%3A9781781911952%7CResourceVersion%3D2019-03-28T22%3A37%3A14Z). Others maintain the figures represent a literal chronology of pre-kingdom judgments, with the same period designated variously as 1,260 days, 42 months, and 3½ years[3](logos4-command:TextRangePreview%7CRanges%3D1137626%3A336%3AeyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJyZXNvdXJjZUlkIjoiTExTOkdSVE5TU0tOR0RNS05HRCIsInJlc291cmNlVmVyc2lvbiI6IjIwMjMtMDctMjBUMTQ6Mjk6NTZaIiwiaW5kZXhlZE9mZnNldCI6IjExMzc2MjYiLCJpbmRleGVkTGVuZ3RoIjoiMzM2IiwibmJmIjoxNzkxOTUwNzgxLCJleHAiOjE3OTE5NTQzODEsImlhdCI6MTc5MTk1MDc4MSwiaXNzIjoiVGV4dFJhbmdlQWNjZXNzVG9rZW4iLCJhdWQiOiIxMzkxNjE3MiJ9.F43Rn4CKRFxs1wSzEvzFQ3_vuPcT-ajI8AlSg1iFXjo%7CResourceId%3DLLS%3AGRTNSSKNGDMKNGD%7CResourceVersion%3D2023-07-20T14%3A29%3A56Z).
