“ *I counsel you to buy from me gold refined by fire, so that you may be rich, and white garments so that you may clothe yourself and the shame of your nakedness may not be seen, and salve to anoint your eyes, so that you may see.* “
 [Re 3:18](https://ref.ly/logosres/esv?ref=BibleESV.Re3.18&off=3&ctx=lind%2c+and+naked.+18%C2%A0~I+counsel+you+v%EF%BB%BFto+b)
I did not know what this was referring to so, I put the verse into Logos and prompted with who is this to and, it gave me the response of:

```
This counsel is directed to the church in Laodicea ([Rev 3:1–22](https://ref.ly/Re3.1-22)). The message addresses a community whose spiritual condition is characterized as lukewarm—neither fully committed nor openly resistant—and whose members falsely believe themselves to be materially secure and spiritually complete ([Rev 3:1–22](https://ref.ly/Re3.1-22)).

The speaker offers three remedies: precious metal refined through fire to replace their false sense of wealth, garments to cover their spiritual nakedness, and eye salve to restore their spiritual vision ([Rev 3:1–22](https://ref.ly/Re3.1-22)).

```

Which makes sense, not to fall victim of pride in external that cover the spiritual state.

This seems Jesus is referring to the conquering of the flesh and the world, over coming sin in His name.
