Daniel 9:1-19 is a prayer from Daniel to God. How sweat are the sounds that are to God of praise and worship, humbling ourselves to the glory of God. The words Daniel uses are so beautiful; knowing and acknowledging that we are sinners and in need of a saviour, Daniel gets to the heart of the matter and lays it all out. I can definitely learn from the way Daniel prays for the people and for himself.

           I lifted up my eyes and looked, and behold, a man clothed in linen, with a belt of fine gold from Uphaz around his waist. 
           6       His body was like beryl, his face like the appearance of lightning, his eyes like flaming torches, his arms and legs like the gleam of burnished bronze, and the sound of his words like the sound of a multitude. (Da 10:5-6)

This seems very similar to the description that John has in Revelation of Jesus, it is unclear, however this encounter if its is Jesus, then is another example of how Jesus transcends time and shows His divinity and power.

	And he said, “O man greatly loved, fear not, peace be with you; be strong and of good courage.” And as he spoke to me, I was strengthened and said, “Let my lord speak, for you have strengthened me.” (Da 10:19)

Gabriel speaks to Daniel, and later, what sounds like Jesus speaks to Daniel, and Daniel hears these words. This gives me motivation to keep pressing towards God, as I think about how wonderful it would be to hear words like this from the maker of heaven and earth.
