	Now Elisha had said to the woman whose son he had restored to life, “Arise, and depart with your household, and sojourn wherever you can, for the LORD has called for a famine, and it will come upon the land for seven years.” (2 Ki 8:1)
	Restore all that was hers, together with all the produce of the fields from the day that she left the land until now.” (2 Ki 8:6)
Time passed between the two verses; she was told to leave and return, and to trust God. This seems simple, but putting it into daily life isn’t easy. To lean on God is against our nature; we want to take control, believing that we can do anything and everything. Society shouts at us more and more every day that we are kings of our destiny and we must make our future, and being a good steward of what God has given us is to work hard and be wise; the heart of the matter is: where is that trust? There is a point where you either say God I trust you, or God, I don’t. I say God I trust you, what will you say?
