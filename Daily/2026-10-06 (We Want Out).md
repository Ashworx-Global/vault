Through the commentary from : [Commentary Critical and Explanatory on the Whole Bible](logos4-command:Resource%7CId%3DLLS%3A29.1.27%7CLinkToSourcePanel%3Dtrue%7CReference%3Dbible.18.34.1%7CTooltipKind%3DInfo)Commentary

**29.** _Margin,_ “twice and thrice,” alluding to Job 33:14; once, by visions, Job 33:15–17; secondly, by afflictions, Job 33:19–22; now, by the “messenger,” thirdly, Job 33:23.

 Robert Jamieson, A. R. Fausset, and David Brown, [_Commentary Critical and Explanatory on the Whole Bible_](https://ref.ly/logosres/jfbcomm?ref=Bible.Job33.29&off=0&ctx=Ps+56%3a13%3b+Ec+11%3a7\).%0a~29.+Margin%2c+%E2%80%9Ctwice+a), vol. 1 (Oak Harbor, WA: Logos Research Systems, Inc., 1997), 337.

Seems a often pattern with Job and potentially in our lives, is the communication or verification from God through more than one method, so to say “ I heard from God” needs to be verified in another sense, like someone confirming or a word coming in the morning readings coupled with events that align with people and so on, as to simple “ Hear from God” could very well be just your random thoughts.
