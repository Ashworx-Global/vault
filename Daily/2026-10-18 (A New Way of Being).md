These typically announce impending judgment, such as the destruction of Samaria in 722 BC and Jerusalem in 586[1](logos4-command:TextRangePreview%7CRanges%3D135325%3A179%3AeyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJyZXNvdXJjZUlkIjoiTExTOjk3ODE0MzM2ODM3NzAiLCJyZXNvdXJjZVZlcnNpb24iOiIyMDIyLTAyLTA5VDE5OjIwOjAwWiIsImluZGV4ZWRPZmZzZXQiOiIxMzUzMjUiLCJpbmRleGVkTGVuZ3RoIjoiMTc5IiwibmJmIjoxNzkyNjQ3NjM2LCJleHAiOjE3OTI2NTEyMzYsImlhdCI6MTc5MjY0NzYzNiwiaXNzIjoiVGV4dFJhbmdlQWNjZXNzVG9rZW4iLCJhdWQiOiIxMzkxNjE3MiJ9.X7HHpffBGeZVSLoLC7c8WbMfCvlzcfTA_MmXEL32HdM%7CResourceId%3DLLS%3A9781433683770%7CResourceVersion%3D2022-02-09T19%3A20%3A00Z), though they sometimes concern the downfall of enemy nations, offering encouragement to God’s oppressed people[1](logos4-command:TextRangePreview%7CRanges%3D135504%3A147%3AeyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJyZXNvdXJjZUlkIjoiTExTOjk3ODE0MzM2ODM3NzAiLCJyZXNvdXJjZVZlcnNpb24iOiIyMDIyLTAyLTA5VDE5OjIwOjAwWiIsImluZGV4ZWRPZmZzZXQiOiIxMzU1MDQiLCJpbmRleGVkTGVuZ3RoIjoiMTQ3IiwibmJmIjoxNzkyNjQ3NjM3LCJleHAiOjE3OTI2NTEyMzcsImlhdCI6MTc5MjY0NzYzNywiaXNzIjoiVGV4dFJhbmdlQWNjZXNzVG9rZW4iLCJhdWQiOiIxMzkxNjE3MiJ9.yGv5EWSvIlnn9DKC7AkIbL9bwmfnMBnc19Lr9syBipY%7CResourceId%3DLLS%3A9781433683770%7CResourceVersion%3D2022-02-09T19%3A20%3A00Z). Importantly, near-term prophecies carried a conditional quality—repentance could defer or delay the announced judgment[1](logos4-command:TextRangePreview%7CRanges%3D135651%3A294%3AeyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJyZXNvdXJjZUlkIjoiTExTOjk3ODE0MzM2ODM3NzAiLCJyZXNvdXJjZVZlcnNpb24iOiIyMDIyLTAyLTA5VDE5OjIwOjAwWiIsImluZGV4ZWRPZmZzZXQiOiIxMzU2NTEiLCJpbmRleGVkTGVuZ3RoIjoiMjk0IiwibmJmIjoxNzkyNjQ3NjM3LCJleHAiOjE3OTI2NTEyMzcsImlhdCI6MTc5MjY0NzYzNywiaXNzIjoiVGV4dFJhbmdlQWNjZXNzVG9rZW4iLCJhdWQiOiIxMzkxNjE3MiJ9.Vq0WAguouN4r6PzKQgckp3Y-OsKhVAsq39wSX-nPbsw%7CResourceId%3DLLS%3A9781433683770%7CResourceVersion%3D2022-02-09T19%3A20%3A00Z).

The terminology reflects the timeframe of fulfillment rather than the content of the prophecy itself.
