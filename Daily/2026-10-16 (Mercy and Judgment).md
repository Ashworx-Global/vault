This almost sounds like what Jesus said on the cross.
So I asked Logos and this is what I got:
The phrase “It is done!” in [Revelation 16:17](https://ref.ly/logosref/bible$2Besv.87.16.17) translates the Greek word **γέγονεν** (_gegonen_), the perfect tense form of the verb _ginomai_ (γίνομαι), meaning “to become” or “to happen.”

This is the same Greek word Jesus uses on the cross in [John 19:30](https://ref.ly/logosref/bible$2Besv.64.19.30) when he says “It is finished!” (_Tetelestai_—a different verb, but conveying similar finality). In [Revelation 16:17](https://ref.ly/logosref/bible$2Besv.87.16.17), the perfect tense emphasizes that God’s work of judgment is now **complete**—the action has been accomplished and its effects remain. The word carries weight beyond mere completion; it signals the culmination of divine purpose.

So it could be Jesus that is saying the pouring out of the Judgement is complete and sore has been settled, we have been given grace from the father, Jesus stood in the gap, and fulfilled the requirement.
