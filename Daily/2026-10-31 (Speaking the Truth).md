In Daniel 11, we get a glimpse into the heart of greed. As the king grows in strength, he focuses all his attention on himself and to the end he falls. In today’s time, you can see this play out in real time if you have a look at the way politicians do their business. Self-adoration and pure, unadulterated greed that is so obvious to everyone else except the person themselves. To bring this a little closer, do we act like this in other areas, like our time and attention?

	And someone said to the man clothed in linen, who was above the waters of the stream,2 “How long shall it be till the end of these wonders?” (Da 12:6)

Is this another event of Jesus revealing Himself in the Old Testament? It's unclear, but it could be, as the next verse seems to indicate.

      And I heard the man clothed in linen, who was above the waters of the stream; he raised his right hand and his left hand toward heaven and swore by him who lives forever that it would be for a time, times, and half a time, and that when the shattering of the power of the holy people comes to an end all these things would be finished. (Da 12:7)

Daniel is given peace and a promise at the end; he has been faithful and is given the promise of heaven. We have been given the promise of heaven if we accept Jesus as Lord. If you have not yet, I plead for you to accept Jesus as Lord, as He died and rose on the cross for your sins, past, present and future, so that you to may be in heaven and get to be in the glory of God for all time.
