     When you walk, they will lead you; 
      when you lie down, they will watch over you; 
      and when you awake, they will talk with you.(Pr 6:22)
  I never saw this before, the way that this is worded is like its pointing to the Holy Spirit. Jesus said that He is leaving and will send a Helper who will be with us always. 
**The three main verbs in this verse are:**

- **”Lead” (נחה /** **_nachah_****)** — means to guide, conduct, or direct someone along a path
    
- **”Watch over” (שׁמר /** **_shamar_****)** — means to guard, protect, or keep watch; it’s the same word used for God “keeping” or “preserving” His people
    
- **”Talk with” (דבר /** **_dabar_****)** — means to speak, converse, or communicate; it’s the common Hebrew verb for speaking
The Hebrew pronoun **הֵם** (_hem_) is the third-person masculine plural form meaning “they.” It functions as the subject of all three verbs in the verse—leading, watching over, and talking with you. The personification treats these teachings as active agents accompanying you throughout your day.

So it seems that it is not a foreshadowing of the Holy Spirit, but of the Word, however the Word is God, and the Holy Spirit does speak through the Word and it does give a indication of how the Holy Spirit does speak.
