That is a hard pill to swallow, but the Lord said it so I must listen and believe what it says and submit to it. Ezekiel 3 talks of if you are a righteous person then you are obligated to speak to others of righteousness else their blood is on your hands. Father God give me the wisdom and courage to speak your Word to others.

It seems in Ezekiel 4 there is punishment to the city of Israel for what they have done, punishment is due when you do not follow the Word and turn your eyes from God. This carries on to Ezekiel 5, there are real consequences for not obeying God and defiling His law and places of worship. 
Head this, God is Lord and obeying Him is the natural order of things, and when that is not done, everything falls apart. Father God help me to follow you, Holy Spirit work in me to align my heart to the Word, thank you, Jesus the Son, for making the way possible for saving me.
