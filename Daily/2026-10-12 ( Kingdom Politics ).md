God is not some fairy tale, but a active person in our lives, not to be taken granted. He is a loving father and as a father will reprimand His children when they do wrong.

“ *you shall not mourn or weep, but…* “ -  [Eze 24:23](https://ref.ly/logosres/esv?ref=BibleESV.Eze24.23&off=71&ctx=shoes+on+your+feet%3b+~you+shall+not+mourn+)
This must have been a hard thing to do, but Ezekiel did as he was told to, when his wife passed away and was told to continue his work and not mourn her, I am sure he prayed for Heavenly strength to do that, Father help me to do your will even when it is hard, in your strength I can do what you ask of me, in Jesus name I pray.

“ *For thus says the Lord God: Because f_you have clapped your hands and stamped your feet and rejoiced with all the malice within your soul against the land of Israel, “ - [Eze 25:6](https://ref.ly/logosres/esv?ref=BibleESV.Eze25.6&off=2&ctx=at+I+am+the+Lord.+6%C2%A0~For+thus+says+the+%E2%80%A2L)*
