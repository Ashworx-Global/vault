When he brought me there, behold, there was a man whose appearance was like bronze, with a linen cord and a measuring reed in his hand. And he was standing in the gateway (Eze 40:3)

I was thinking that the person is an angel but using Logos bible app I looked up who is this person :
The figure in [Ezekiel 40:3](https://ref.ly/logosref/bible$2Besv.26.40.3) is an angel[1](logos4-command:TextRangePreview%7CRanges%3D17955825%3A220%7CResourceId%3DLLS%3AINTLBBLDICT1915%7CResourceVersion%3D2023-09-20T01%3A31%3A49Z)—a heavenly messenger sent to conduct Ezekiel through a visionary tour of the future temple. This being appears with bronze-like features and carries measuring instruments (a linen cord and measuring reed) while standing at the temple’s gateway. The bronze appearance distinguishes this figure from another divine guide Ezekiel encounters earlier in his visions, suggesting they are separate beings[2](logos4-command:TextRangePreview%7CRanges%3D4838761%3A233%7CResourceId%3DLLS%3AFSB%7CResourceVersion%3D2025-07-30T17%3A17%3A47Z).

The angel’s role centers on precise documentation. He instructs Ezekiel to observe carefully and report all measurements and details to Israel ([Ezek 40:3–4](https://ref.ly/logosref/bible$2Besv.26.40.3-26.40.4)). The measuring tools serve distinct purposes—the linen cord for longer distances and the reed for shorter measurements[2](logos4-command:TextRangePreview%7CRanges%3D4838994%3A176%7CResourceId%3DLLS%3AFSB%7CResourceVersion%3D2025-07-30T17%3A17%3A47Z). This meticulous measuring symbolizes exactitude in the temple’s dimensions, reflecting the symmetry and perfection of God’s design
