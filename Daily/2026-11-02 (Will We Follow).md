     Be strong, and show yourself a man, 
           3       and keep the charge of the LORD your God, walking in his ways and keeping his statutes, his commandments, his rules, and his testimonies, (1 Ki 2:2-3)

Here we have it in plain words how to be a man. To be a man is to keep charge of the Lord. If you are not fearing the Lord and striving to keep the things that God has said us to do then you are not yet a man. 

The events in this chapter can seem rather grim; however, two main themes I can see are: one, that God sets the order, Solomon rights some wrongs that were set against David and, by extension, the Lord; and two, when grace was given and then abused by Shemei, ignoring the promise he made, judgment is handed to him. I can be convinced that keeping your word and aligning my thoughts and desires with God are the right and pure things to do, not to act “holy” out of fear of God, but out of reverence for His majesty.

	And rising very early in the morning, while it was still dark, he departed and went out to a desolate place, and there he prayed. (Mk 1:35)

Jesus understood morning devotion and quiet time, this is the time when the Holy Spirit gets to fill your spirit up and give you the ability from the Father to deal with the attacks for the day.

	And when Jesus saw their faith, he said to the paralytic, “Son, your sins are forgiven.” (Mk 2:5)

Jesus gets to the heart of the problem: the outside the was crippled, the inside that no one could see was that he needed forgiveness. Father, I need forgiveness, and I thank you for your Son, that through the name of Jesus I can boldly come to your throne in forgiveness.

     But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins”—he said to the paralytic— 
           11       “I say to you, rise, pick up your bed, and go home.” (Mk 2:10-11)

This is the altimate flex here, Jesus is the creator of everything, and the creation is pointing a fingure at the creator, and He simply proves the point of who is in charge.

     And he said to them, “The Sabbath was made for man, not man for the Sabbath. 
           28       So the Son of Man is lord even of the Sabbath.” (Mk 2:27-28)

Looking at the sabbath as an act in itself is against what it means; the day of rest was given to man to enjoy the presence of God and reflect on His majesty, enjoy fellowship and praise Him. The act, in a different way, is religion, which can be an idol in our lives.
