It almost seems that here the description of man has been laid out in the description of Jerusalem, and what they have done. How society has fallen and the chasing of self and doing whatever is needed to achieve the idol of ones self, the “get ahead” so so paralleled here. Clearly anything that is not God is an idol and should be broken and removed, does not mean we cannot have nice things but God should be the first priority, then through our love for Him, express our love my achieving these other goals that help others see that He is our first love and is the provider of all.
