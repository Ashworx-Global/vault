*For they themselves report concerning us the kind of reception we had among you, and how you turned to God from idols to serve the living and true God (1 Th 1:9)*

The Greek word *eidōlon* for idols, which carries two main distinctions, the first is the obvious which is physical trinkets like carved figures but also carries the distinction of any false god, so any belief other than Yahweh.

So turning from the way of doing and living for other gods, of which one is self. Worshiping self is putting your interest above God, whether it is feeling sorry for yourself or thinking that you are the most important person in the world, they both are worshiping yourself.

Bending your knee to God, is submitting everything to Him and rooting my identity in who He says I am and His will be done.

The people in this world like to say things that are blasphemous or think that they are above God, so saying that “when I get to heaven I want to say or ask…” these thoughts are rooted in self and are impure.
