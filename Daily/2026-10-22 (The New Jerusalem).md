As the glory of the Lord entered the temple by the gate facing east, 
**5** the Spirit lifted me up and brought me into _m_the inner court; and behold, the glory of the Lord filled the temple. (Eze 43:4-5)

If the temple is a picture of the relationship with God and His people, then how beautiful is this picture or vision of God flowing into that space.

Only the prince may sit in it to eat bread before the Lord. He shall enter by way of the vestibule of the gate, and shall go out by the same way. (Eze 44:3)

When I read this, I recall the words of Jesus, “I am the way, the truth and the light, no one comes to the Father except through me”, I wonder if this imagery is intended to showcase Jesus coming.

God sets the stage of how to be set apart, this is similar to the way it is expressed in Deuteronomy with Moses. We are to be set apart from the rest of the world, to keep ourselves away from sin and things that can lead us to sin. The act of deliberately living for God is what stands out for me in this reading.
