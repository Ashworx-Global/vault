Jesus is in control, nothing is of chance, in the garden of Gethsemane, this interaction can seem odd, but it shows so much of the character and nature of God and Jesus. Jesus shows His humanity in full view,
*And going a little farther, he fell on the ground and prayed that, if it were possible, the hour might pass from him.  - Mk 14:35*, here we can openly see the weight of what is happening to Jesus, emotions and feelings. Feelings are good guides but make terrible lords, so we see the answer to feelings, And he said, *“Abba, Father, all things are possible for you. Remove this cup from me. Yet not what I will, but what you will.” - Mk 14:36*. Lets look at two things here, Abba is a very intimate word here for father, the only way that Jesus could say that is to know the Father in heaven ( 1 to 1 ), and then your will, this shows how to take feelings and emotions, to the Father.

After all these things Jesus says, *The Son of Man is betrayed into the hands of sinners. Rise, let us be going; see, my betrayer is at hand.”  - Mk 14:41-42*, this is not someone that was surprised by the events happening to Him, this is someone that knows the will of the Father. 

We need to daily read the word, ponder and think about the word and what it means in our lives, speak and pray to the Father constantly, and act when promoted by His spirit, doing this grows our relationship with the Father.

      And when he came, he went up to him at once and said, “Rabbi!” And he kissed him (Mk 14:45)
Judas could not resolve his heart to Jesus being the messiah, the Saviour, Son of God; he could only muster Rabbi, teacher, so many people view Jesus as some good speaker, good person or just a good teacher. I proclaim Jesus as Lord over my life and pray that you too proclaim Him Lord of your life, because He is Lord and my saviour and your saviour; every knee will bow, either as judge or saviour. I urge you to make Him Lord.

Growing up, something that was always on display for me was hard work, from my mother always busy around the house and often “recruiting us” or my father working hard to build an environment of security for our well-being. Work has never been something that has been lacking in my life, and that gem has shaped my life in such a profound way, because when you take to heart the words of Solomon here, and you apply them, something that is not always seen will happen; faith is fostered in the ability to know what it means to work, because there is a connection between faith and the result, and that is work. Work in step with God, and faith carries you through so much. My exposure to work has allowed me to openly see that faith as work can be the vehicle that the light shines on your heart to keep going.
