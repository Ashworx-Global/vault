	For am I now seeking the approval of man, or of God? Or am I trying to please man? If I were still trying to please man, I would not be a servant of Christ. (Ga 1:10)
Paul gets to the heart of the question here: where is your heart? Jesus said the same thing, so we know Paul's words are driven by the Holy Spirit and written down for the church in Galatia, and for you and me reading this today. Who is lord over your heart? If it's anything but Jesus, then there is a problem. Think of that question as a good litmus test for behaviour and decisions.

	On the contrary, when they saw that I had been entrusted with the gospel to the uncircumcised, just as Peter had been entrusted with the gospel to the circumcised (Ga 2:7)
God knows each of us and what He created us for, so to compare ourselves to each other is foolish. Having a relationship with the Almighty and asking Him to show you, in your area, that we should be His children is the most personal and worship-filled way to build that relationship with the Father, as when we do that and walk in the gifts that He gave us, fulfilment is always guaranteed.

	Christ, so we also have believed in Christ Jesus, in order to be justified by faith in Christ and not by works of the law, because by works of the law no one will be justified. (Ga 2:16)
So in today’s terms what can this mean, well lets address some. Must you go to church? No, but you should, as it is good to worship God with other believers, hear sound teachings from the Bible and build relationships with believers that can help you turn to Jesus in difficult times. There are several social thoughts like that which demand certain rules which make having a relationship with God seem distant, like God is some sort of drill sergeant that is waiting for you to step out of line, only to smack you with the ruler and call you back into line; this is far from the truth. God is sovereign; make no mistake, He has a side that has wrath, but the loving Father, the Abba Father, attributes of God are shown constantly in the Bible, and I have been so fortunate to see moments in my life that radiate with this side of God and have seen His love poured out in my life.
