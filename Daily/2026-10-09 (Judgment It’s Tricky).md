“ *But they rebelled against me and were not willing to listen to me. None of them cast away the detestable things their eyes feasted on, nor did they forsake the idols of Egypt.* ” - [Eze 20:8](https://ref.ly/logosres/esv?ref=BibleESV.Eze20.8&off=2&ctx=e+Lord+your+God.%E2%80%99+8%C2%A0~h%EF%BB%BFBut+they+rebelled+)

This seems to a common theme with us. Father God, help me to keep my eyes on you, my heart is for you, but my natural reaction is to fall in the traps of this would that take my eyes off you. Work in my heart and soften my heart for those who do not follow you, give me the peace that you are in control and I should follow your lead Father, as your will be done.

Back and forth God and the people of Israel move between Grace and Wrath, as the people fall trap of the ways of people around them, even offering children up as sacrifices. The struggle is real and is the same today as it was then. Comfort, convenience, complacency and a sense to no oppress some else’s feelings are all signs of the same spirits today as it was then, Father God help me to put the full armor on, and stand firm on your Word, to resist the temptations of the evil one, that so easily disguises these modern ideals as truths and let me fall to your work that shows them up as falsehoods as they are. Your will Father, ready my heart for you and where needed let me act in your will to rectify these falsehoods to bring glory to your name.
